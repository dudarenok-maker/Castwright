## Results

### Text level (re-confirmation)
- `candidate.sourceText` head codepoints: `4C 2019 C9 74 E9 … 2014 4E 65`
  — real `’`,`É`,`é`, leading em-dash `—`; **zero residual `&…;` markup**
  (regex sweep for every entity used came back clean). PASS.
- Caveat worth recording: `candidate.chapters[0].title` came back as the
  **NCX navLabel** ("L'Ete", plain ASCII as authored in `nav.ncx`), not the
  entity-bearing `<h1>` — chapter-title selection prefers the TOC label, so a
  fixture whose nav label lacks entities cannot text-prove title decode via
  that field. The `<h1>` decode is proven by the `sourceText` head.

### Audio level (the owed gap)

| `Ne partez pas après le dîner, dit-elle.` (same text, dash removed — control) | `Ne partez pas après le dîner, dit-elle.` | identical transcript |

**Dash-pause timing evidence** (dash vs identical no-dash control, 24 kHz
mono, whisper word-timestamps + ffmpeg `silencedetect n=-35dB:d=0.12`):

- total duration **3009 ms with dash vs 2315 ms without** (+694 ms);
- clause-boundary pause before `dit-elle`: **340 ms with dash vs 60 ms
  control** (+280 ms);
- per-word onsets all longer in the dash take (`Ne` 180 vs 140 ms,
  `dîner` 400 vs 280 ms).

Interpretation: XTTS v2 realizes a leading dialogue dash as a **prosodic
break/stretch, not as leading digital silence** — no silence segment starts
at t=0 in either take (the first silence in the dash take is the 1497 ms
clause pause). The register's "renders with a pause (not spoken 'ampersand …
em-dash semicolon')" criterion is met on both halves: a measurable ~340 ms
pause is inserted and the dash is never vocalized — the transcript is
character-exact against the decoded sentence.
