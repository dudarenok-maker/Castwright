## Step 5 — dash-opened dialogue attribution: **not exercisable on this fixture**

The row asks to confirm that dash-opened dialogue lines (a paragraph that
*opens* with the language's dash marker and carries no quote glyph at all —
the convention `attribution-health.ts`'s `dashOpenedPara` / `conv.dialogueOpen`
checks, the same one `stage2-coverage.ts`'s "dash-opening speech lines" count
and the analyzer's own narrated-speech-check log line report) are attributed
as dialogue rather than falling to the narrator.

**`the-coalfall-commission.ru.md` contains zero such paragraphs.** Every line
of dialogue in the fixture is wrapped in guillemets (`«…»`), with the dash
used only *inside* the quotes as a mid-sentence attribution tag
(`«Оставь, — сказал мастер Одуван, … — Кто бы это ни был, пусть стучит».`) —
never as the sole marker opening an unquoted paragraph. This was independently
confirmed two ways:

1. **Server log, both chapters, verbatim:** "*source has 0 dash-opening
   speech lines*" (chapter 1 and chapter 2 narrated-speech-check lines both
   say this).
2. **Direct inspection of the fixture**: `grep -n "^—"` against
   `the-coalfall-commission.ru.md` returns no matches — no paragraph in the
   file begins with a bare dash.

The three sentence-level fragments in the attributed output that *start*
with "—" are not paragraph-opening dash markers; they are mid-paragraph
continuations after an inline attribution tag, still inside the guillemet
convention (from `.audiobook/manuscript-edits.json`'s `sentences`):

| id | ch | attributed to | confidence | text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2 | mawrin | 0.65 | `— Кто бы это ни был, пусть стучит».` |
| 15 | 2 | mawrin | 0.80 | `— Мэйрин не улыбнулась.` |
| 28 | 2 | dragon | 0.65 | `— В деревнях меня зовут Коалфолл. Это не моё имя, но сойдёт.` |

None of these fell to the narrator either — but that is not evidence for
the row's actual claim (which is specifically about the *bare-dash, no-quote*
convention), and two of the three are themselves misattributed by this small
model relative to the source (sentence 4 is Oduvan's line, attributed to
Mawrin; sentence 15 is a narrator beat — "Mawrin did not smile" — attributed
to Mawrin as if she said it). Reporting this as observed model behavior, not
claiming it as the row's pass condition.
