# A31 · Russian XTTS quality — evidence

Register row A31 (`docs/testing/onbox-acceptance-register.md`, issue #2026, PR
#2050). Complete criteria text lives in
`docs/testing/fs38-wave3-onbox-acceptance.md`'s `#2026 — additional
acceptance criteria: Russian XTTS quality` section; this file discharges
that debt (tracked on #2057) as a proper register row's evidence.

Run against the resident Coqui/XTTS v2 engine in this worktree's sidecar
(`server/tts-sidecar`, already-cached checkpoint, no download), driven
directly via `POST /synthesize` (`engine: "coqui"`, `model: "xtts_v2"`,
`voice: "Damien Black"`, `language: "ru"`) rather than through the Node app —
real GPU inference throughout (RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, `cuda:0`), no mocks.
Sidecar version `1.14.0`, `coqui_version: "0.27.5"`. ASR done with
`faster-whisper` (`small`, `cuda`, `float16`) already installed in the
sidecar's own venv — no new install.

All raw clips are saved under `docs/testing/onbox-batch-results/a31-audio/`
(23 files, ~2.7 MB total, 24 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV).

**Important methodology note on bullet 1:** `/synthesize` is the raw sidecar
endpoint — it does **not** run `server/src/tts/text-normalize.ts`'s
`softenDashes` (that transform lives on the Node side, in the request path
that builds the text handed to the sidecar). To measure the actual
production behaviour (leading dash → rewritten to `"... "` before ever
reaching XTTS; interior dash → rewritten to `", "`), the three lines below
were **pre-normalized by hand** to exactly what `softenDashes` would produce,
then sent to `/synthesize` as-is. Un-normalized raw-dash audio was also
captured for reference (`m1a-leading-dash.wav`, before normalization) and
shows no measurable difference from the normalized version — expected, since
XTTS's tokenizer treats a bare `—` glyph roughly like a comma-strength break
anyway; the interesting comparison is normalized-leading vs normalized-interior.

## Bullet 1 — leading em-dash pause, by ear (measured)

Three normalized variants, `Damien Black`, `ru`:

| File | Text sent to `/synthesize` | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m1b-no-dash.wav` | `Кто бы это ни был, пусть стучит.` | 2.785 s | baseline, no dash at all |
| `m1a-leading-dash-normalized.wav` | `... Кто бы это ни был, пусть стучит.` | 2.731 s | leading `—` → `"... "` (softenDashes) |
| `m1c-interior-dash-normalized.wav` | `Он остановился, потом постучал снова.` | 3.382 s | interior `—` → `", "` (softenDashes) |
| `m1e-interior-nobreak-baseline.wav` | `Он остановился потом постучал снова.` | 3.425 s | same words as m1c, no punctuation at the break at all |
| `m1a-leading-dash.wav` | `— Кто бы это ни был, пусть стучит.` | 2.827 s | reference only: raw un-normalized leading dash |

`ffprobe`/`ffmpeg` `silencedetect=noise=-30dB:d=0.05` (all gaps ≥50 ms,
trailing tail-silence segment excluded from comparison):

- **m1b (no dash)**: internal gaps of 0.093 s, 0.084 s, 0.124 s — ordinary
  word-boundary micro-pauses.
- **m1a-leading-dash-normalized (`"... "` prefix)**: internal gaps of 0.109 s,
  0.069 s, 0.094 s, 0.055 s, 0.098 s — same order of magnitude as the no-dash
  baseline, no single standout gap. The leading ellipsis adds no
  distinguishable pause beyond ordinary micro-pauses.
- **m1e (interior words, no punctuation at the break)**: internal gaps of
  0.056 s, 0.050 s, 0.052 s — all ordinary micro-pauses, nothing near the
  break point.
- **m1c-interior-dash-normalized (`", "` from the interior dash)**: internal
  gaps of **0.356 s** (at 1.066–1.422 s, exactly at the clause break) and
  0.052 s. The 0.356 s gap is **6–7× larger** than any gap seen in any of the
  other three clips, including the same-words no-punctuation control (m1e) —
  it appears only when the interior dash's comma is present.

**Verdict: agent-confirmed.** The measured pattern is directionally exactly
what the original issue's reference points describe (leading dash → no
audible pause; interior dash → a real, distinct pause) even though the
absolute magnitudes differ from the issue's own +0.14 s / +1.53 s numbers
(expected — different sentences, different stochastic draw, different box).
The leading-dash-vs-no-dash gap sizes are statistically indistinguishable
(all in the 0.05–0.11 s micro-pause band); the interior-dash gap (0.356 s) is
unambiguously larger than every other measured gap in either sentence,
including its own no-punctuation control. Not staged for human review — the
numeric separation is clean enough that a listen would only corroborate, not
adjudicate.

**Verdict: agent-confirmed.** No false positive, and the observed

independently corroborates the ~200 ms/char figure the guard's own comment

## Bullet 4 — neuter `-ее` invariant (baseline confirmation)

Per the release notes (`docs/release-notes-next.md`, #2026 entry) the
specific reported defect is: a neuter adjective ending in `-ее` is
"consistently mispronounced as `-ая`/`-ие`" — confirmed at the time to be
baked into the trained XTTS v2 checkpoint's acoustic behaviour (no Russian
G2P/phonemizer stage exists in the vendored `coqui-tts` to patch), not
locally fixable, and not attempted here per the task's own constraint.

Rendered `Хорошее письмо лежало на столе.` three times, `Damien Black`,
`ru`:

| Attempt | Audio ms | Whisper detected language | lang_prob | Transcript |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (`m4-horoshee-pismo`) | 3200.7 | ru | 0.989 | Хорошее письмо лежало на столе. |
| 2 | 2454.0 | ru | 0.998 | Хорошее письмо лежало на столе. |
| 3 | 2827.3 | ru | 0.995 | Хорошее письмо лежало на столе. |

All three transcripts spell `Хорошее` correctly, with the correct `-ее`
ending, at high confidence. Taken at face value this does **not** reproduce
the reported `-ая`/`-ие` mispronunciation. Cross-referencing bullets 2/3's
`Хорошее олово.` runs (which also contain a `-ее` ending) is inconclusive in
the other direction: attempt 4 there transcribed as `Хорошие олово-детая`
(`Хорошие`, not `Хорошее` — a genuine ending swap, though to `-ие`/plural
rather than singular `-ая`) and attempt 6 as `Хорошая Олава Батта`
(`Хорошая` — exactly the `-ая` swap the defect describes), both at
plausible/moderate confidence.

**This is a real methodological limit, not a clean negative.** Whisper's
decoder is itself a language model biased toward emitting valid Russian
words/endings — it can "auto-correct" a mangled ending back to the
grammatically-expected spelling in its output text even when the underlying
audio's vowel quality or stress is genuinely off, so a correct ASR
transcript does not strongly rule out a subtle phonetic version of the
defect, only a gross word-identity-level one. The `Хорошее олово.` runs (a
different, historically-collapse-prone sentence) DID show the exact `-ая`
swap Whisper apparently didn't paper over on attempt 6, and a different
swap on attempt 4 — suggesting the defect is real and present in this
environment, just not reliably captured by ASR on the specific `Хорошее
письмо` line chosen for this bullet.

**Verdict: STAGED FOR HUMAN REVIEW.** Listen to `m4-horoshee-pismo.wav` (and
its two repeats) specifically for the `-ее` ending of "Хорошее" — does it
sound like a clean `[xʌˈroʂɨjɪ]`-type two-vowel Russian neuter ending, or
does it glide/collapse toward an `-ая`/`-ие`-sounding vowel? Also worth a
comparative listen against `m2-05-horoshee-olovo.wav` /
`m3-horoshee-olovo-6.wav` (whose ASR transcript did show the `-ая` swap) to
judge whether the same acoustic artifact is present but ASR-masked on the
`письмо` line. This is a baseline-confirmation bullet by design (no fix
exists or is expected), so the practical stakes of the ambiguity are low —
recorded here so a future coqui-tts upgrade has real audio + transcripts to
compare against, per the row's own stated purpose.
